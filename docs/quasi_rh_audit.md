## Consequences and limits for factoring

For every nonprincipal character, a zero must have real part at most 7/8. For a real zero beta, 1-beta >= 1/8, uniformly even in the conductor. Thus any exceptional-conductor deletion defined solely by a zero with real part >7/8 has an empty exceptional set. This eliminates the *zero-location* obligation in the deterministic manuscript's near-one conductor argument. It does not by itself prove the corrected Perron bound, the uniform saddle expansion, clean squarefree smooth progressions, the two-prefix estimates or randomized weighted progression contracts. To replace the manuscript's analytic proof, a uniform bound on L'/L on an interior line (e.g. re(s)=15/16), with conductor and height dependence, must still be established and inserted into the truncated explicit formula. The principal pole, Euler factors of imprimitive characters, and endpoint error require explicit treatment. No new factoring complexity bound follows merely from the nonvanishing theorem.

A narrow source-level adapter and a reproducible build are provided separately from the verified core. The present runtime lacks Lean and outbound Git access; new adapters must not be counted as verified until the pinned build and axiom audit succeed.
